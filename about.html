Who we are

Advocacy, empowerment and evidence — for Northern Nigeria.

LPDI promotes literacy, girl-child education, disability inclusion, Almajiri system reform and community development, working to reduce educational inequality and improve social outcomes across the region.

Executive summary

A non-profit built on a simple premise.

LPDI is a non-profit organisation established to promote literacy, girl-child education, disability inclusion, Almajiri system reform, and community development across Northern Nigeria. The organisation seeks to reduce educational inequality and improve social outcomes through advocacy, empowerment, partnerships, and evidence-based interventions.

Vision

To build an educated, productive, inclusive and prosperous society where every child, youth and vulnerable individual has access to quality education and empowerment opportunities.

Mission

To promote literacy, educational access, social inclusion, disability empowerment and sustainable reforms through advocacy, partnerships, community engagement and evidence-based interventions.

Strategic goal

Equitable access, stronger teachers, real inclusion.

To increase equitable access to quality, inclusive and sustainable learning opportunities for out-of-school children, while strengthening community support, teacher capacity, and the socioeconomic inclusion of people living with disabilities.

One-Year Strategic Action Plan

Our targets.

The four priorities that anchor LPDI's first year of programme delivery.

I.

Strengthen community ownership and advocacy for education through structured engagement.

II.

Identify, mobilise and support the enrolment of out-of-school children, with attention to girls, Almajiri children and children with disabilities.

III.

Build teachers' capacity in learner-centred teaching, inclusive education, classroom management, safeguarding and basic psychosocial support.

IV.

Support the empowerment, participation and inclusion of people living with disabilities through skills development, linkages and community-based opportunities.
