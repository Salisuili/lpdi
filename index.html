Our mission

To promote literacy, educational access, social inclusion, disability empowerment and sustainable reforms through advocacy, partnerships, community engagement and evidence-based interventions.

Every programme LPDI runs traces back to one strategic goal: equitable access to quality, inclusive, sustainable learning — backed by stronger teachers, engaged communities, and real pathways for people living with disabilities.

Our vision & values
What we're working toward

Seven objectives, one region.

From girl-child enrolment to Almajiri reform, digital literacy to disability inclusion — each objective responds to a specific, named gap in Northern Nigeria.

Girl-Child Education

Raising awareness and enrolment for out-of-school children, with a direct focus on girls.

Disability Empowerment

Skills training and job placement for persons living with disabilities, reducing street begging.

Almajiri System Reform

Advising on a restructured Almajiri system to curb out-of-school risk, banditry and social decay.

Digital Literacy

ICT training and modern equipment for public schools, driving innovation-led growth.

One-Year Strategic Action Plan

How we get there.

Our targets guide the first phase of work — from community ownership to teacher capacity to disability inclusion.

Read the full strategy
I.

Strengthen community ownership and advocacy for education through structured engagement.

II.

Identify, mobilise and support enrolment of out-of-school children — girls, Almajiri children and children with disabilities.

III.

Build teachers' capacity in learner-centred teaching, inclusive education, classroom management and safeguarding.

IV.

Support empowerment, participation and inclusion of persons living with disabilities through skills and linkages.
