Aim & objectives

Seven objectives, each answering a named gap.

LPDI's work is organised around seven concrete objectives — from enrolment to disability inclusion, health outreach to policy advocacy.

01

Bridge the educational gap for girls

To bridge the educational gap in northern states by raising awareness about the importance of girl-child education and increasing enrolment of out-of-school children.

02

Reduce street begging through PLWD empowerment

To reduce the prevalence of street begging in the northern region by empowering persons living with disabilities (PLWD) with necessary skills and facilitating their employment in both public and private sectors.

03

Advise on restructuring the Almajiri system

To properly address and advise on the kind of restructuring the Almajiri system needs to alleviate societal moral decadence, the out-of-school menace and banditry — promising a solid future for a growing youth population.

04

Organise periodic medical programmes

To organise periodic medical programmes that address the health needs of vulnerable groups, including children, pregnant women, the elderly and persons living with disabilities.

05

Promote digital literacy & technology

To promote digital literacy and technological advancement in the Northern region by providing ICT training programmes, equipping public schools with modern technology, and supporting innovation-driven initiatives that enhance education, employment and economic development.

06

Highlight critical infrastructure needs

To identify and highlight critical areas in need of urgent infrastructural development, urging northern leaders to take action.

07

Participate in policy-making & partnerships

To actively participate in policy-making and collaborate with northern leaders, the federal government and NGOs to achieve common goals.

One-Year Strategic Action Plan

Our four targets.

These translate the seven objectives into a working first-year plan.

I.

Strengthen community ownership and advocacy for education through structured engagement.

II.

Identify, mobilise and support the enrolment of out-of-school children, with attention to girls, Almajiri children and children with disabilities.

III.

Build teachers' capacity in learner-centred teaching, inclusive education, classroom management, safeguarding and basic psychosocial support.

IV.

Support the empowerment, participation and inclusion of people living with disabilities through skills development, linkages and community-based opportunities.

Strategic goal

Equitable access to quality, inclusive, sustainable learning — for every child.
